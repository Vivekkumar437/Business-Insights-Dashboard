# Business-Prediction-Dashboard
The Business Insight Dashboard is an interactive Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze sales performance, product category trends, customer segments, regional performance, and profitability metrics.

The dashboard transforms raw business data into meaningful visual insights, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales patterns, and make data-driven business decisions.

Built using Microsoft Excel, the dashboard combines interactive charts, KPI cards, and slicers to provide a consolidated view of business performance.

***
The primary objectives of this project are to:

**Analyze sales performance across different product categories and regions.
**Evaluate product sales distribution across customer segments.
**Compare revenue contributions from Business, Individual, and Wholesale customers.
**Examine profit margin and discount rate distribution across product categories.
**Understand the relationship between sales amount and quantity sold.
**Enable interactive analysis through filters for payment method, customer segment, sales channel, and city.
